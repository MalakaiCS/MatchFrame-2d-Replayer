| Asset | Why | What is needed |

## Map calibration values

Origins and scales in `web/src/shared/maps.json` are the published CS2 overview parameters for each map. They are factual configuration values, not images. Each map stays `calibrated-unverified` until three landmarks per floor have been checked (see `docs/replayer-parity.md`, MAP-*).

## Reference product

Skybox EDGE is a third-party product. Its screenshots, recordings or walkthroughs are used only to identify functionality during the parity audit. Its layout, visual identity, icons and assets must not be copied.
