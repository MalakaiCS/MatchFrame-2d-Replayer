## Map calibration values

Origins, scales and floor splits in `web/src/shared/maps.json` equal Valve's overview files (`data/radar_info/<map>.txt` in the radar source repository) for every map. They are factual configuration values.

Seven maps are `verified` by a play-coverage check: 99.91–100% of live-player positions from real GOTV demos land on the radar's playable area, on every floor (`worker/tools/radar_alignment.py`). This replaces the three-landmark check with a far denser one. Overpass, Train and Vertigo keep the official values but await a real demo.

## Reference product

Skybox EDGE is a third-party product. Its screenshots, recordings or walkthroughs are used only to identify functionality during the parity audit. Its layout, visual identity, icons and assets must not be copied.
